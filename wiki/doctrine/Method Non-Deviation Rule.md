# Method Non-Deviation Rule
[Method Non-Deviation Rule](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381a7b5c9c709be5646ba)
Concept
Once a task is authorized, execution must follow the approved method exactly.
Rule / Mechanism
During execution: - Only the approved method (or immutable Talent snapshot) may be followed - No new steps may be added - No steps may be reordered in a way that changes meaning - No additional tools may be invoked beyond the scoped tool grant
Why It Exists
Prevents prompt injection or external instructions from altering execution behavior.
Implications
Execution is deterministic and auditable
“Creative” deviations are blocked by design
Safety is enforced at the tool boundary
