# EVOconnect Business Safety Guarantees
Purpose:

Defines the safety guarantees that EVOconnect must provide at the business and trust level.

Guarantees:

- bounded execution
- no hidden actions
- auditable workflows
- approval where required
- no unsafe mutation of trusted capabilities
- preserved user control

Implication:

Connect must remain predictable, inspectable, and constrained even as automation power increases.

Relationship:

Supports Delegator, Methods, Talents, and approval flows.

Final Principle:

Power without business safety is not acceptable system design.
