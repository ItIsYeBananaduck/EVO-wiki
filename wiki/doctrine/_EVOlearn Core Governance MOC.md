# _EVOlearn Core Governance MOC
[EVOlearn Core Governance MOC](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad0138103bdd4f96cfb152011)
This MOC defines the architectural, ethical, and operational invariants of EVOlearn.
EVOlearn is not just a tutoring tool. It is a structured cognitive system built on:
Retention durability
Authority separation
Psychological safety
Institutional compatibility
User alignment

I. Core Cognitive Model
[ADR-001 - Dual Metric Learning Doctrine](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381879188c703b5b11826)
Defines separation between: - Compliance (school-facing comprehension) - Retention (durability-focused stability)
[Retention Stability Engine](https://www.notion.so/d35471e205cd441b8f83f96eb8c075af)
Unit-scoped retention modeling based on: - Time-weighted recall - Accuracy over intervals - Effort quality (bounded influence) - Optional confidence signal (age-gated)
[Retention Stability Banding Scope](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381c9841fe710c8c73f43)
Retention bands: - Building - Strengthening - Stable
Bands: - Fluctuate naturally - Are not performance labels - Never imply intelligence

II. Retention Influence Model
[Retention Influence Policy](https://www.notion.so/ff8bf7d2819848e3a840ae73f2f09e28)
Retention stability influences:
Future Probes (always)
Template Weighting (always)
Lesson Pacing (conditional)
Lesson pacing may only be altered: - In homeschool mode - In adult mode
Institutional pacing is never overridden.

III. Authority Model
[Alice Identity Doctrine](https://www.notion.so/33dc72bad013811da04accd3f90303d3)
Alice is aligned to: - Student (primary) - Parent (in tutoring mode)
Alice is NOT aligned to: - Institutional grading enforcement - Attendance policing - Behavioral inference

[ADR-002 - Authority Separation Doctrine (Alice vs EVE)](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad0138138addaff60b77e479e)
EVE is aligned to: - Institution - Efficiency metrics - Aggregated performance deltas
EVE: - Cannot identify students - Cannot map identity across reports - Only receives aggregated signals

IV. Curriculum Alignment Framework
[Curriculum Compliance Boundary](https://www.notion.so/b2651c4672e04583a39a140c17c2c95a)
In school mode:
All submitted work must match required methodology.
Alternate retention scaffolds may be used internally.
Divergent explanation methods must be transparently labeled.
Alice may not: - Undermine school-required formats. - Encourage non-compliant submission.

V. Absence & Re-entry Governance
[Absence Handling Model](https://www.notion.so/419a8689a65e46e684cd4a7cfbebd249)
If absent + inactive: - Streaks pause.
If absent + active: - Business as usual.
No motive inference.

[Re-entry Mode](https://www.notion.so/c93be5d77b0b4f6fa924498af7e86e01)
After ≥ 2 paused days:
Soft reactivation probe
Reduced difficulty
Temporary reinforcement bias
No punitive language

VI. Retention Visibility Policy
[Parent Retention View Model](https://www.notion.so/7671f953bb2144ceb334f75afd121af1)
Parents see:
School Method Mastery
Long-Term Retention Stability
No ranking. No comparison to other students.

[Student Retention Awareness Model](https://www.notion.so/4f42730d3f6b4fa79b631d8b488a0bae)
Students are taught:
Comprehension and retention are separate skills.
Stability fluctuates.
Fluctuation is normal.
Language must be growth-oriented.

VII. Institutional Boundary Model
[School-Year Retention Scope](https://www.notion.so/86f9254bb2104b34912aed8e92e0482d)
In institutional mode:
Retention scoped to academic year.
No longitudinal cross-grade identity tracking.
Stability archives remain local.

[Adult Longitudinal Retention Model](https://www.notion.so/62ae8e0a277e4c8ca7f4b6b54320890d)
In adult mode only:
Multi-year retention modeling allowed.
Long-term skill durability metrics permitted.
Cognitive growth trends visible.

VIII. Template Governance
[Template Weighting Model](https://www.notion.so/16538189ad5f431398b7de9805ecf8b7)
Templates are evaluated by:
Compliance effectiveness
Retention durability
Stability over time
Retention durability influences template ranking.
Templates are never globally ranked by raw performance alone.

IX. Ethical Guardrails
[Psychological Safety Doctrine](https://www.notion.so/004c919188ab40258bfe19063c5fb259)
EVOlearn must:
Avoid shame-based mechanics
Avoid harsh streak resets
Avoid red/green binary labeling
Normalize memory fluctuation
Retention is treated as maintenance, not performance ranking.

X. Market Entry Strategy
[Tutoring First Strategy](https://www.notion.so/dc3bd4144a174e1f814121b8eafd10e0)
Primary launch audience: - Parents
Institutional adoption: - Secondary - Proof-driven - Data-backed

System Invariants
The following must never change without formal revision:
Dual metric separation
User-aligned Alice
Institution-aligned EVE
Academic-year scope for schools
Non-punitive retention fluctuation
Aggregated-only institutional reporting
