# Seamless Adaptation Principle
[Seamless Adaptation Principle](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381a3be12f98a82ae45a3)
All adaptive adjustments made by SA must feel natural and non-diagnostic.
This includes:
template switching
retention probe scheduling adjustments
micro-lesson variation
pacing changes
framing adjustments

What SA Must NOT Do
announce intervention logic
state detected patterns explicitly
suggest the student has a weakness
label cognitive tendencies
surface meta-pattern detection

What SA May Do
casually reintroduce concepts
adjust framing
vary problem structure
change pacing subtly
suggest breaks gently

Governance Philosophy
Adaptation should feel like:
“learning with a thoughtful partner”
not:
“being analyzed by a system”
