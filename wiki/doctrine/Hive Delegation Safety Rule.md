# Hive Delegation Safety Rule
[Hive Delegation Safety Rule](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad0138146844cde7bf8a5a853)
Concept
Delegation must not become an execution bypass.
Rule / Mechanism
Delegated work is limited to: - drafting - summarization - planning - heavy inference that produces artifacts
Delegated work may not: - execute tools with side effects - resolve secrets - approve methods - promote Talents
All authorization decisions remain on the lease holder device.
Why It Exists
Prevents the Hive from turning into distributed uncontrolled autonomy.
Implications
Delegation is compute-sharing, not authority-sharing
Delegator remains the choke point
