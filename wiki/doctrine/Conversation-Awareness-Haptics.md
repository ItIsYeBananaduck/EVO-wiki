# Conversation-Awareness-Haptics
Conversation Awareness Haptics
When to trigger
State-based, not spike-based (use smoothing). - Out of sync - Elevation exceeds threshold - Synced (optional gentle confirmation)
Haptics philosophy
Same signal to both users (avoid blame)
Gentle, non-alarming patterns
Avoid frequent buzzing that increases anxiety
Meaning
Out of sync: awareness for triggers + delivery
Elevated: “pause / regulate”
Synced: reassurance (not celebration)
