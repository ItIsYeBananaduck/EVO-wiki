# _MOC - Privacy
[_MOC - Privacy](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381869ceaec42fb583a22)
Privacy doctrine for EVOlearn. Student identity must never be reconstructable from analytics data.
Governed by: - ADR-002 - Authority Separation Doctrine (Alice vs EVE) - EVOlearn Constitution
Core Privacy Rules
No student mapping keys
Rolling IDs per report cycle
Aggregated-only reporting
Escalation data siloed from analytics
