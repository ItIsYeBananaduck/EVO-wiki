# Live Activity Inference
[Live Activity Inference](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381c78f2ff2ab2e8dab34)
Concept
Live activities allow ongoing intelligence without foreground UI.
Rule / Mechanism
During live activities: - Inference is allowed - UI updates are minimal - Interruptions are suppressed unless safety-critical
Why It Exists
Users may be active without actively looking at the app.
Implications
Pocket-safe operation
Workout-aware intelligence
Battery-conscious behavior
