# EVE Safety Constraint Model
Purpose:

Define non-negotiable safety constraints across the system.

Constraints:

- prevent harmful or unsafe outputs
- enforce domain-specific limits
- block unsafe execution paths

Principle:

Safety constraints must be enforced before execution, not after failure.
