# Milestone-Architecture
Milestone Architecture
Two tiers:
Micro Achievements
Frequent
Encouraging
Non-shareable
Do not affect Alice’s form
Adaptive Phase Milestones
Rare
Earned through mesocycle completion
Shareable
Trigger subtle Alice evolution
Milestones are tied to system coherence, not streaks.
