# EVOlearn Lesson Execution Flow
Purpose:

Defines how a lesson or learning session runs.

Flow:

- present content
- check understanding
- capture response signals
- adjust explanation or challenge level

Principle:

A learning session should be adaptive, guided, and easy to follow.
