# EVO — Subscription & Capability Philosophy (Raw Draft)

## Purpose

This note defines the high-level monetization and capability philosophy of the EVO ecosystem.

The EVO subscription model is designed to:
- preserve local ownership
- preserve user agency
- avoid artificial AI crippling
- monetize operational infrastructure rather than intelligence itself
- align pricing with real infrastructure and orchestration costs

The core philosophy is:
> users should own and use their local AI freely, while advanced orchestration and ecosystem infrastructure remain premium operational systems.

---

# Core Principle

EVO does not monetize the existence of Alice.

EVO monetizes:
- orchestration
- distributed infrastructure
- persistent operational systems
- advanced automation
- ecosystem evolution
- large-scale coordination

The subscription model must never feel like:
> “pay to unlock your own AI.”

Instead, it should feel like:
> “unlock the full operational ecosystem.”

---

# Local Ownership Philosophy

Users own:
- their local Alice
- their local models
- their local adapters
- their local workflows
- their local notes
- their local tasks
- their local orchestration state

Local intelligence should remain usable without subscription lock-in.

The free experience must still feel:
- powerful
- useful
- agentic
- operational
- complete enough to demonstrate the ecosystem philosophy

---

# Insight vs Orchestration

The ecosystem generally separates capabilities into two layers:

## Insight Layer
Free-friendly capabilities.

Examples:
- local chat
- observations
- recommendations
- analysis
- lightweight workflows
- manual orchestration
- local Talents
- local reasoning
- local adaptive suggestions

These systems help the user understand and operate.

---

## Orchestration Layer
Primarily Pro capabilities.

Examples:
- distributed execution
- persistent orchestration
- autonomous delegation
- advanced automation
- background workers
- Hive coordination
- aggregate evolution
- advanced adaptive systems
- long-running workflows
- unattended orchestration

These systems actively operate on behalf of the user.

---

# Connect Philosophy

Connect is the most operationally powerful EVO application.

Because of this, Connect requires special monetization philosophy considerations.

Connect Free must still allow:
- local agentic workflows
- local Talents
- local pane systems
- local Alice operation
- manual orchestration
- local execution
- task management
- lightweight automation

This preserves the philosophy that:
> the user owns and operates their local AI.

Connect Pro primarily unlocks:
- distributed Hive orchestration
- autonomous node delegation
- persistent orchestration
- background workflows
- advanced multi-node execution
- operational infrastructure scaling

The distinction is:
- Free = local operational intelligence
- Pro = distributed operational intelligence

---

# Hive-Oriented Monetization

The Connect Pro model is fundamentally based around Hive orchestration.

Free users:
- may own multiple nodes
- may sync nodes
- may share visibility across nodes

But:
- nodes do not autonomously delegate execution between each other
- unavailable tasks remain pending until the user reaches a capable node
- worker-node orchestration remains disabled

Pro unlocks:
- autonomous delegation
- smart node bidding
- distributed execution
- worker-node participation
- anchor-node orchestration
- persistent orchestration workflows
- unattended execution

This monetizes:
- orchestration complexity
- distributed infrastructure
- coordination systems

rather than local intelligence itself.

---

# Global Adapter Philosophy

Global adapters are ecosystem-level intelligence distributions.

They are distinct from:
- local adapters
- personal adapters
- user-owned learning systems

Global adapters represent:
- aggregate ecosystem learning
- shared operational improvements
- ecosystem-wide optimization patterns

Users always retain:
- their local learning
- their local adapter evolution
- their local operational patterns

Subscriptions affect:
- the frequency of ecosystem intelligence updates

not the ownership of local intelligence.

---

# Global Adapter Distribution Philosophy

Free users may receive:
- infrequent aggregate updates
- slower ecosystem evolution
- long-cycle global refinement

Pro users may receive:
- current aggregate updates
- rapid ecosystem improvements
- latest global operational refinements

Potential cadence examples:
- Free = every 6–12 months
- Pro = monthly or rolling updates

This allows EVO to:
- reduce infrastructure costs
- reduce unnecessary distribution overhead
- align costs with active ecosystem usage

while still ensuring all users benefit from ecosystem evolution over time.

---

# Operational Infrastructure as Premium Value

The primary premium value of EVO is not:
- AI existence
- basic local intelligence
- local workflows

The premium value is:
- orchestration scale
- persistent systems
- advanced automation
- ecosystem coordination
- distributed operational intelligence
- continuous ecosystem evolution

This aligns monetization with:
- actual infrastructure cost
- engineering complexity
- orchestration capability
- operational value

# Anti-Crippling Rule

EVO must avoid:
- artificially crippling local intelligence
- paywalling basic ownership
- disabling core local operation
- creating dependency on cloud execution for basic functionality

The ecosystem should always preserve:
- user empowerment
- local-first operation
- ownership
- privacy
- operational freedom

Premium systems should enhance operational scale rather than restrict baseline ownership.

---

# Long-Term Direction

The long-term direction of EVO subscriptions is:
- local-first ownership
- distributed operational infrastructure
- scalable orchestration
- evolving ecosystem intelligence
- optional advanced coordination systems

The ecosystem should feel:
- empowering
- user-owned
- operationally scalable
- collaborative

rather than restrictive or artificially gated.
