# Template-Execution-Rules
Template Execution Rules
Boundaries
Templates: - may not change lesson objectives - may not override unlock rules - may not introduce off-curriculum topics in Kids mode - must respect teacher-defined allowed_adaptations
Composition
Templates may be: - used standalone within a section - chained inside a Talent - suggested by Teacher Alice - auto-selected by Student Alice within allowed bounds
Authority
Teacher approval required to: - add new template to class - modify allowed adaptations - adopt new global template permanently
