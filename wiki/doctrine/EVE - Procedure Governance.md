# EVE - Procedure Governance
[EVE – Procedure Governance](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381b689b6c74f7ec35061)
Procedures define:
Ingestion scope
KPI calculations
Aggregation level
Reporting format
LoRA log channel
Only administrators may create or modify Procedures.
Department heads and managers may: - View reports - Request new procedures - Propose changes
They cannot directly modify Procedure definitions.

Rationale
Procedures shape organizational metrics. Metric drift creates governance instability.
Administrative control ensures: - Consistency - Accountability - Auditability - Political neutrality
