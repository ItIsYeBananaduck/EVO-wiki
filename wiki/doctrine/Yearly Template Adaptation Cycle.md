# Yearly Template Adaptation Cycle
Yearly Template Adaptation Cycle (SA)
SA adapts year over year.
Each academic year: - starts with a warm prior (what worked last year) - increases exploration early in the year - revalidates template effectiveness in new contexts - stabilizes once patterns are confirmed

Phase 1 – Warm Start
Load prior template weights from previous year.
Use as initial bias only.
No permanent locking.

Phase 2 – Exploration Window
During first N weeks or first M concept clusters: - increase template exploration rate - test new templates where relevant - evaluate subject-specific differences

Phase 3 – Revalidation
Templates are revalidated per subject and concept cluster: - retention stability - time-to-mastery - support intensity - template-switch frequency
Weights adjust quickly early.

Phase 4 – Stabilization
After revalidation: - reduce exploration - prioritize proven templates - still explore when stall signals occur

New Templates
New templates enter as candidates: - tested during exploration window - tested on stall events - tested when teacher unlocks scope-specific templates
