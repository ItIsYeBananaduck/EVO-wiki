# Weekly Adaptive Review Specification
Generated: Sunday Night Displayed: Monday Morning
Structure: 1. Performance State 2. Trend Watch 3. What I Noticed 4. What I Adjusted 5. This Week’s Focus 6. User Interaction Options
Tone: - Analytical - Calm - Encouraging - Non-judgmental - Safety-first
User may: - Request deeper explanation - Override recommendation - Adjust goals
Domain Firewalls
Training → Mind
Training data may influence tone and recovery suggestions. Training data may not interpret emotional meaning.
Mind → Training
Stress volatility may influence load adjustments. Conversation awareness data may NOT influence training models.
Conversation Awareness
Ephemeral.
No long-term relational training.
No cross-domain reinforcement.
