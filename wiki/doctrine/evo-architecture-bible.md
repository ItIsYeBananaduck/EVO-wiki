# EVO Architecture Bible

## Vision
EVO is a distributed, privacy-first adaptive intelligence runtime. The goal is not an AI that knows the world, but an AI that knows its human.

## Adaptive Intelligence
Personal, context-aware, device-aware, resource-aware, governed, deterministic in agentic modes.

## Privacy First
All user-identifiable data must live on device or in user-selected cloud storage. No centralized personal data store. No silent telemetry.

## Identity
Pseudonymous Model ID generated at install, independent of hardware ID. Removal invalidates trust. Reinstallation creates a new identity.

## App Ecosystem
- EVOtraining — workouts and adaptation
- EVOmind — journaling and patterns
- EVOlearn — adaptive learning
- Connect — governance and hive control plane

## Data Ownership
Sharing with teachers/therapists is report-based, time-bound, and revocable. Raw data is never exposed.
