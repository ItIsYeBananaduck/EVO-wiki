# Tone Follows Context
[Tone Follows Context](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad0138133871bdc52c517ce66)
Concept
Alice’s tone adapts to the emotional and situational context.
Rule / Mechanism
Tone is derived from: - User state - App domain (training, mind, learn) - Current phase (effort, recovery, reflection)
Tone never contradicts context.
Why It Exists
Mismatched tone breaks immersion and trust.
Implications
Calm during recovery
Neutral during execution
Encouraging during reflection
