# Escalation + Unlock Flow
Learn – Escalation + Unlock Flow
If approved templates fail AND student effort is high:
Alice may offer escalation request.
Student chooses: - Share summary - Or decline summary
If declined: System still reports student + concept tag (not Alice reporting).
Teacher reviews: Conducts 1:1 Unlocks additional templates
Message to student: “New learning approach available.”
