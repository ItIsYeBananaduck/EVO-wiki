# Daily-Pack-Preload
Daily Pack Preload
Goal
Ensure students can learn at home even with poor internet.
When
At end of school day OR at scheduled sync window
What gets cached
next unlocked lesson content
optional next 1–2 lessons (locked but downloaded)
review set (spaced repetition items)
practice variants (generated from templates)
teacher notes allowed for students (optional)
Size control
hard cap per day (configurable)
prioritize text + lightweight media
defer large video unless explicitly required
