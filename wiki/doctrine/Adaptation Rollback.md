# Adaptation Rollback
[Adaptation Rollback](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad013811b9ff7c068f1f43526)
Concept
All adaptations must be reversible without penalty.
Rule / Mechanism
If a change produces negative signal, the system reverts to the previous stable state.
Rollback does not trigger further reduction unless risk persists.
Why It Exists
Users should not fear honest feedback.
Implications
Encourages compliance
Prevents cascading regressions
Builds long-term trust
