# Escalation Triggers
Purpose:

Defines when execution must escalate beyond ordinary handling.

Trigger Examples:

- ambiguity or uncertainty rises beyond threshold
- privileged action is requested
- external agent authority would expand
- safety boundary may be crossed
- approval is required and not yet granted
- mutation risk increases

Relationship:

Supports Delegator, task supervision, and escalation packet flows.

Final Principle:

Escalation is not failure. It is the system choosing governance over guesswork.
