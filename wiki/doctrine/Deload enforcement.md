# Deload enforcement
[Deload Enforcement](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381c9af60d641482bf50e)
Concept
Recovery phases must be enforced, not suggested.
Rule / Mechanism
When a deload is scheduled or required, progression is suppressed.
Load, volume, or intensity reductions are mandatory.
Why It Exists
Users consistently underestimate accumulated fatigue.
Implications
Long-term adherence improves
Injury risk decreases
Performance rebounds are stronger
