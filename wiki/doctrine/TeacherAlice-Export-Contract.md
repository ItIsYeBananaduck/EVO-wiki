# TeacherAlice-Export-Contract
Teacher Alice Export Contract
What TA can export to SAs
lesson objectives
key concepts and definitions
examples and practice sets
pacing recommendations
teaching patterns (tone, structure, sequencing)
allowed explanation styles (visual, story, step-by-step, game-template)
constraints (what not to teach, what to avoid)
What TA must NOT export
any student data
any student performance history
any private teacher notes about specific students
any relational conclusions
Format
Exports must be structured: - JSON schemas - tagged concept graphs - leveled content variants
