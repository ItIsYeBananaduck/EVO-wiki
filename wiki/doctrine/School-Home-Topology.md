# School-Home-Topology
School / Home Topology
School Environment
School-issued device is the primary EVOlearn node.
EVOlearn is Learn-only (domain-limited).
Receives curriculum packages from Teacher Alice.
Stores student learning progress for classroom use.
No cross-student swarm pooling on campus.
Home Environment
Student personal devices pair to the student’s Learn instance.
Personal devices can provide per-student swarm compute at home.
Home devices can generate:
additional practice variants
simplified explanations
template-based micro-games
review sessions for retention
Personal devices may run full EVO ecosystem, but EVOlearn context stays Learn-scoped.
