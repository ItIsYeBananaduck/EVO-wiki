# Unlock-Rules
Unlock Rules
Lesson Completion Criteria (default)
A lesson unlocks the next lesson when: - student completes required activities, AND - mastery signal meets threshold OR has stabilized upward
Mastery Signal (non-punitive)
uses trend across attempts
allows mistakes
prioritizes “can succeed with decreasing help”
does not punish slow learners
Teacher Controls
Teacher can: - set thresholds per lesson - choose time-based gating (unlock on date) - choose mastery-based gating (unlock on mastery) - choose hybrid (earliest date + mastery)
Student Experience
never shows “failed”
shows “not yet”
offers alternate methods automatically
