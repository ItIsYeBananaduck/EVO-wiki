# EVOtraining Warm-up & Capacity Detection
Purpose:

Determine safe working capacity.

Process:

- begin with machine-based warm-up
- gradually increase load
- monitor heart rate and fatigue
- identify safe upper bound

Principle:

Capacity is discovered, not guessed.
