# Approved Method Library
## Purpose

Define how approved Methods are stored and reused before they are promoted into Talents.

---

## Core Principle

> Approved Methods reduce repeated planning work.

> They do not grant autonomous execution.

---

## Concept

When a user approves a Method for a task and that task executes successfully, the Method may be stored in the Approved Method Library.

The Approved Method Library exists to:

- preserve useful approved Methods
- reduce repeated planning effort
- support structured reuse without creating autonomy

---

## Reuse Behavior

Approved Methods are reusable references that may be attached to future tasks.

However:

- reuse does not inherit approval
- each new execution still requires explicit approval
- only Talents remove the need for per-task re-approval

This means:

- Approved Methods accelerate planning
- Approved Methods do not authorize execution

---

## Relationship to Delegator

Delegator may point Alice toward an existing approved Method when a future task resembles a previously approved execution pattern.

This supports:

- faster task setup
- less repeated reasoning
- continued bounded execution

Delegator does not treat Approved Methods as Talents.

---

## Relationship to Talents

Approved Methods are not Talents.

They remain:

- non-autonomous
- approval-bound
- reusable only through explicit user authorization

An Approved Method may later become a Talent if it satisfies the promotion requirements defined in the Talent Promotion Rule.

---

## Final Principle

> Approved Methods preserve useful structure.

> Talents preserve trusted execution.

The Approved Method Library exists to reduce repeated work without reducing control.
