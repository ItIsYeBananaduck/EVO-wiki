# Connect Philosophy
Purpose:

Defines the philosophical basis of EVOconnect.

Core Themes:

- governed execution
- user-aligned automation
- structured rather than chaotic agency
- transparent rather than hidden action
- scalable without surrendering control

Final Principle:

Connect should amplify action without dissolving oversight.
