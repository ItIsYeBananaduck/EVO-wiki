# EVOmind Reflection Loop
Purpose:

Defines how EVOmind creates continuous personal insight.

Loop:

- capture thoughts (journaling)
- reflect on entries
- identify patterns over time
- surface insights
- adjust behavior or perspective

Principle:

Awareness compounds when reflection is structured and continuous.
