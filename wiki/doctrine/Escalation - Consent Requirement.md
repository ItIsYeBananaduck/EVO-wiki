# Escalation - Consent Requirement
[Escalation – Consent Requirement](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381c69e63fa9dcd5a5207)
Escalation is never automatic.
Even if all criteria are met, SA must request student consent.

Rationale
preserves agency
avoids surveillance feel
reinforces effort
builds trust

Student Options
Approve escalation
Try another approach
Defer escalation
Escalation requires explicit approval.
