# Escalation Packet
Purpose:

Defines the structured bundle passed during escalation.

Packet Contents:

- task or issue summary
- current context
- attempted steps
- relevant constraints
- reason for escalation
- requested decision or action

Role:

Keeps escalations auditable, structured, and easy to review.

Relationship:

Paired with escalation triggers, Delegator, and task supervision.

Final Principle:

An escalation should carry context, not just confusion.
