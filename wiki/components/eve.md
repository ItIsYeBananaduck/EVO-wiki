# EVE — Adaptive Coaching Runtime

## Role
EVE is the adaptive execution surface for coaching workflows. It converts methods and templates into runtime behavior.

## LoRA Strategy
EVE distinguishes between generic adapters and starter adapters. Validating domain fit before deployment prevents cross-contamination between training and coaching contexts.

## Procedure Execution
Procedures are task graphs with:
- explicit scheduler model
- report request routing
- override guardrails
- execution lease compatibility

## Scheduling
EVE procedures are schedulable events with:
- district envelope management
- session-level policy constraints
- mesocycle awareness for training continuity
