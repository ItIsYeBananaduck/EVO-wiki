# Safe Bridge Rules

## Allowed sharing
Teacher → Student Alice:
- skill tags
- mastery levels (low/med/high)
- recommended focus areas
- pacing flags (behind/on-track/ahead)

## Not allowed
Student → Teacher:
- student private profile content
- journaling content
- emotional content
- personal interests outside learning (unless explicitly opted in)

## Principle
Student Alice may use both channels to personalize learning.
Teacher must not access Channel A.
