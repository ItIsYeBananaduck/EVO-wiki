# Volume Adjustment Logic
[Volume Adjustment Logic](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381bcbfccc068ef943130)
Concept
Volume is the safest lever for adaptation when recovery is uncertain.
Rule / Mechanism
Volume (sets) is adjusted before load when: - Fatigue is rising - Performance is flat - Recovery signals are mixed
Why It Exists
Volume changes are easier to tolerate and reverse than load changes.
Implications
Safer mid-mesocycle tuning
Better adherence
Reduced burnout
