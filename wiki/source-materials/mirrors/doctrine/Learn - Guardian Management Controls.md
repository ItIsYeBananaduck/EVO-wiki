# Learn – Guardian Management Controls

Guardian controls apply to Kids Self-Study mode when enabled/required.

Controls may include:
- allowed subject categories
- blocked categories
- ingestion sources (camera-only vs files vs both)
- session time limits
- schedule windows (e.g., after school)
- export/sharing restrictions

Guardian management should feel supportive, not punitive.

SA messaging:
- neutral and calm
- explains restrictions as safety rules, not judgment
- offers allowed alternatives when blocked
