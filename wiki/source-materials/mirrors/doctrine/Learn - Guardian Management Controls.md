# Learn - Guardian Management Controls
[Learn – Guardian Management Controls](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad013812a99eedac4099ef8f1)
Guardian controls apply to Kids Self-Study mode when enabled/required.
Controls may include: - allowed subject categories - blocked categories - ingestion sources (camera-only vs files vs both) - session time limits - schedule windows (e.g., after school) - export/sharing restrictions
Guardian management should feel supportive, not punitive.
SA messaging: - neutral and calm - explains restrictions as safety rules, not judgment - offers allowed alternatives when blocked
