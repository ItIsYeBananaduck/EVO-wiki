# Awareness vs Learning Boundary
Purpose:

Separates what the system may notice in the moment from what it is allowed to learn durably.

Core Principle:

Awareness is not learning. Presence is not permanent mutation.

Boundary Rules:

- awareness may guide immediate behavior
- learning must follow explicit pathways
- ordinary interaction must not silently rewrite durable capability
- persistent change requires governed promotion paths

Relationship:

Connected to:
training mode, Methods, Talents, and privileged execution.

Final Principle:

A system that notices everything must not learn everything.
