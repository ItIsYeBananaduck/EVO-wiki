# Method Is Mandatory
[Method Is Mandatory](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381cb8c97fc4afc6ac233)
Concept
Every task must have an explicit method.
Rule / Mechanism
A task cannot exist without a defined method describing how it would be completed.
Why It Exists
Execution cannot be evaluated, approved, or promoted without a concrete procedure.
Implications
No vague or implicit tasks
Planning always precedes execution
Safety review is possible
