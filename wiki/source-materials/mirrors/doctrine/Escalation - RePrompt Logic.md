# Escalation – RePrompt Logic

If student declines escalation:

1. SA suggests a break or alternate concept.
2. SA continues support without pressure.
3. SA logs anonymous stall telemetry.

Upon revisit:

If stall signals persist and effort threshold met again:
- SA may re-offer escalation.
- Student consent required again.

Escalation is not re-offered aggressively.
It is tied to persistent evidence.

---

# Rationale

- respects student autonomy
- prevents escalation fatigue
- teaches strategic breaks
- aligns with grading-period reporting
