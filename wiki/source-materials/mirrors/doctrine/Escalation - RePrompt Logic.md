# Escalation - RePrompt Logic
[Escalation – RePrompt Logic](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381479b03cd4b319bf0e4)
If student declines escalation:
SA suggests a break or alternate concept.
SA continues support without pressure.
SA logs anonymous stall telemetry.
Upon revisit:
If stall signals persist and effort threshold met again: - SA may re-offer escalation. - Student consent required again.
Escalation is not re-offered aggressively. It is tied to persistent evidence.

Rationale
respects student autonomy
prevents escalation fatigue
teaches strategic breaks
aligns with grading-period reporting
