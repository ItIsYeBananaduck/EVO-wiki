# Reporting - Statistical Neutrality
[Reporting – Statistical Neutrality](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381349a7ee9d08b60f396)
Escalation priority does not influence reporting severity.

Separation Principle
Escalation: - determines support timing - affects teacher workflow - does not affect metric weighting
Reporting: - based on aggregated performance distributions - statistically neutral - unaffected by escalation count

Rationale
prevents penalizing proactive students
avoids escalation stigma
preserves analytic integrity
separates support system from evaluation system
