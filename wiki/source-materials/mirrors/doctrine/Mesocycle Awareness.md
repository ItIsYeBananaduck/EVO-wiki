# Mesocycle Awareness

## Concept
The system must understand which mesocycle the user is currently in and respect its intent.

## Rule / Mechanism
All adaptations occur within the active mesocycle boundary.

The system may not redefine goals mid-cycle.

## Why It Exists
Mesocycles provide structure, predictability, and recovery planning.

Without awareness, adaptation becomes reactive and chaotic.

## Implications
- Daily performance does not override long-term intent
- Fatigue is interpreted in context
- Deloads are meaningful, not arbitrary
