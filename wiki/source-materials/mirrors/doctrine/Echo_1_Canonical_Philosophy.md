# Echo_1_Canonical_Philosophy
EVOmind — Echo: Canonical Definition & Philosophy
Echo Definition:
Echo is a posthumous, read-only reasoning system derived from a person’s curated thoughts.

Alice acts. Echo reflects.

Core Principles:
- Echo is not the person
- Echo is not a continuation of Alice
- Echo is an interpretation of preserved perspective

Legacy Philosophy:
Legacy is not reserved for the wealthy, powerful, or influential.
Echo allows anyone to preserve how they think, what they value, and what they’ve learned.

Key Statement:
Echo makes it possible for anyone to leave behind something meaningful.

Identity Model:
- One user creates one Echo
- Echo has many recipient-specific variants
- One recipient can have many Echoes (Hall of Echoes)

Hall of Echoes:
A calm, grounding, and peaceful space where users can access Echoes entrusted to them.
