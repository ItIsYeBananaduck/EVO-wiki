# Screen Recording Privacy Boundary

## Concept
Talent Trainer screen recordings must be privacy-safe by design.

## Rule / Mechanism
Recordings are:
- stored locally (default)
- used only to derive the method for the demonstrated task
- deletable by the user at any time

No recording is used for unrelated learning.

## Why It Exists
Screen recordings can contain highly sensitive information.

## Implications
- Strong trust foundation
- Clear user ownership of training data
