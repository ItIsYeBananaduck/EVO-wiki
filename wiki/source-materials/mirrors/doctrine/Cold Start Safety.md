# Cold Start Safety

## Concept
New users must experience predictable, safe behavior before personalization occurs.

## Rule / Mechanism
Global systems dominate until sufficient user data exists.

User-specific adaptations are suppressed during early phases.

## Why It Exists
Early errors destroy trust faster than late improvements build it.

## Implications
- Beginner-friendly behavior
- Reduced injury and burnout risk
- Stable system evolution
