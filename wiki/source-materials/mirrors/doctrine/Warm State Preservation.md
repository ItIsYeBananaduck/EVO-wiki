# Warm State Preservation

## Concept
Alice should remain cognitively ready without full reinitialization.

## Rule / Mechanism
Model state is preserved in a low-energy “warm” mode between interactions.

Full reloading is avoided unless necessary.

## Why It Exists
Cold starts feel sluggish and waste energy.

## Implications
- Faster responses
- Lower power spikes
- Smoother UX
