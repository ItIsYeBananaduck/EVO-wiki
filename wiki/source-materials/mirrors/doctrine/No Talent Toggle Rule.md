# No Talent Toggle Rule

## Concept
Talents must not have an enable/disable state.

## Rule / Mechanism
The system must not provide:
- On/off switches for Talents
- Temporary suspension states
- Conditional enablement logic

Talents exist or they do not.

## Why It Exists
Binary existence avoids hidden execution ambiguity.

## Implications
- Clear mental model for users
- Simplified execution logic
- Safer autonomy guarantees
