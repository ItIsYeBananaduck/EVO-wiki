# Contextual Template Adaptation Model

SA adapts template selection based on contextual effectiveness patterns.

Adaptation is:

- subject-scoped
- concept-aware
- continuously revalidated
- non-permanent

---

## Learning Axes

SA tracks:

- Student × Template
- Student × Subject
- Student × Concept Cluster

Templates are weighted based on:

- time-to-mastery
- retention stability
- support intensity
- template-switch frequency

---

## Safeguards

SA does not:
- label student learning type
- permanently assign a template
- expose template preference to reporting layer

Adaptation is internal and local.
