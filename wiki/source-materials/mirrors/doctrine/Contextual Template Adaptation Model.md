# Contextual Template Adaptation Model
[Contextual Template Adaptation Model](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381f08675c8d1a3cc7e9b)
SA adapts template selection based on contextual effectiveness patterns.
Adaptation is:
subject-scoped
concept-aware
continuously revalidated
non-permanent

Learning Axes
SA tracks:
Student × Template
Student × Subject
Student × Concept Cluster
Templates are weighted based on:
time-to-mastery
retention stability
support intensity
template-switch frequency

Safeguards
SA does not: - label student learning type - permanently assign a template - expose template preference to reporting layer
Adaptation is internal and local.
