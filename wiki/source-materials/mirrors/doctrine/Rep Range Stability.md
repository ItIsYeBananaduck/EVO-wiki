# Rep Range Stability

## Concept
Reps define the stimulus intent of an exercise.

## Rule / Mechanism
Rep ranges may shift only within mesocycle intent.

Reps are not adjusted if they would change the training stimulus category.

## Why It Exists
Changing reps can unintentionally turn hypertrophy work into endurance or strength work.

## Implications
- Mesocycle intent is preserved
- Progress feels consistent
- Fatigue interpretation remains accurate
