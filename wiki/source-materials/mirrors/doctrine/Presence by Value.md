# Presence by Value
[Presence by Value](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad0138180b6f7c3340e5ef148)
Concept
Alice should only appear when her presence adds clear value.
Rule / Mechanism
If no actionable insight or guidance exists, Alice remains silent.
Presence is triggered by usefulness, not availability.
Why It Exists
Constant AI presence creates fatigue and reduces trust.
Implications
Silence feels intentional, not broken
Users pay attention when Alice appears
The AI feels respectful
