
## Concept
User-reported difficulty provides directional signal, not absolute truth.

## Rule / Mechanism
Feedback is interpreted relative to:
- Mesocycle phase
- Recent trend
- Recovery indicators

Single data points never dominate.

## Why It Exists
Humans are inconsistent reporters, especially under fatigue or excitement.

## Implications
- Honest feedback is safe
- Outliers are dampened
- Trust is preserved
