# Teacher Visibility - Performance Deltas Only
[Teacher Visibility – Performance Deltas Only](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad013812fb4c9e4538650639a)
SA maintains template tier states privately (on-device).
TA does not receive: - template IDs - template tier labels (Preferred/Candidate/Avoid) - student method preferences
TA receives only: - aggregated performance deltas - distribution summaries - method-shift counts (no labels)

Allowed Delta Signals (Examples)
retention_delta (pre vs post)
time_to_mastery_delta
support_intensity_delta
method_shift_count
shift_effectiveness_bucket (improved/neutral/worse)

Rationale
Prevents: - student labeling - template-based tracking - teacher ranking dynamics - privacy erosion
Preserves: - institutional trust - student dignity - neutral reporting
