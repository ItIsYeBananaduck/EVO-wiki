# Method Promotion Threshold

## Concept
Methods become Talents only after repeated, proven-safe usage.

## Rule / Mechanism
A method becomes eligible for Talent promotion after:
- 3 successful executions
- each execution required explicit user approval
- no safety violations occurred

Eligibility does not automatically promote the method.

## Why It Exists
Prevents premature autonomy and ensures the method is reliably safe in real use.

## Implications
- Trust is earned through repetition
- The system has a clear, testable promotion rule
