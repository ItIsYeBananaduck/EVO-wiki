# Escalation Criteria Threshold Logic

Escalation is effort-based, not error-based.

---

## Required Conditions

1. Concept tagged
2. Independent attempt made
3. Hint used and retried
4. Micro lesson completed
5. Self-understanding rating submitted
6. At least one approved template attempted
7. Progress stall signal detected
8. Student consent granted

All required before escalation.

---

## Progress Stall Signals

- repeated incorrect answers
- retention probe failure
- time-to-mastery outlier
- high template switch rate

---

## Safeguards

- cooldown per concept
- teacher-defined escalation frequency cap
- no escalation without structured attempt evidence

---

## Priority Classification

Low / Medium / High based on:
- retention stability
- repeated failures
- deadline proximity
- concept clustering
