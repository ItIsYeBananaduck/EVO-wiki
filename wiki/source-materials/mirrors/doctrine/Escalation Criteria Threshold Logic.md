# Escalation Criteria Threshold Logic
[Escalation Criteria Threshold Logic](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad0138107aee8df077ab6849e)
Escalation is effort-based, not error-based.

Required Conditions
Concept tagged
Independent attempt made
Hint used and retried
Micro lesson completed
Self-understanding rating submitted
At least one approved template attempted
Progress stall signal detected
Student consent granted
All required before escalation.

Progress Stall Signals
repeated incorrect answers
retention probe failure
time-to-mastery outlier
high template switch rate

Safeguards
cooldown per concept
teacher-defined escalation frequency cap
no escalation without structured attempt evidence

Priority Classification
Low / Medium / High based on: - retention stability - repeated failures - deadline proximity - concept clustering
