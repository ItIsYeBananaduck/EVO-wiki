# Communication Timing Policy – EVOlearn

## Escalation Timing

Escalations may be created at any time.

Teacher notification respects teacher-defined availability windows.

---

## During School Hours

- Escalations delivered immediately.
- Teacher may respond in thread.
- Structured academic exchange.

---

## Outside School Hours

- Escalation ticket created.
- Ticket queued.
- Teacher notified at next active window.
- Student informed of delay.

---

## Rationale

- Protects teacher boundaries.
- Prevents burnout.
- Preserves student support.
- Maintains academic focus.
