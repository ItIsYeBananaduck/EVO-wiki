# Mesocycle Priority Constraint

## Concept
The active mesocycle has higher priority than short-term user feedback.

## Rule / Mechanism
User feedback may influence variables, but not mesocycle direction.

No single session may override mesocycle goals.

## Why It Exists
Short-term signals are noisy. Mesocycles encode intent.

This prevents goal drift and overreaction.

## Implications
- “Feeling good today” does not cause overreaching
- Fatigue trends matter more than spikes
- Adaptation remains coherent
