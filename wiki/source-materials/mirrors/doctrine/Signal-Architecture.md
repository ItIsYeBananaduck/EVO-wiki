# Alice Signal Architecture

## Signal Types

### Training Signals
- strain trend
- recovery drift
- fatigue accumulation
- performance trajectory
- nutrition alignment

### Mind Signals
- stress prediction confidence
- regulation compliance trend
- mood volatility
- conversation sync state (ephemeral only)

## Integration Rule

Signals can inform tone and timing.
Signals cannot override domain boundaries.

Example:
- If recovery drift is high AND stress volatility is high:
  Alice may soften weekly review tone.
  Alice may suggest lighter load.
  Alice may encourage recovery behaviors.

Alice may NOT:
- interpret relational dynamics
- provide therapy advice
- blend conversation awareness into training predictions
