## Core Principle

> Alice does not decide what to do.  
> She decides when to run what is already allowed.

---

## 🔁 Trigger Sources

All Talent execution originates from one of three sources:

---

## 🧩 1. User-Defined Triggers (Strongest)

Defined explicitly by the user.

---

### Examples

- recurring tasks  
- scheduled actions  
- task-based automation  

---

### Behavior

- always allowed  
- predictable  
- highest priority  

---

### Law

> If the user scheduled it, Alice executes it.

---

## 🧠 2. Suggestive Triggers (Advisory)

Alice suggests actions but does not execute without user approval.

---

### Examples

- “You usually do this task now”  
- “This can be automated”  
- “I can handle this for you going forward”  

---

### Behavior

- appears in chat  
- requires confirmation  
- helps user discover automation  

---

### Law

> Suggestions expand capability, not enforce it.

---

## 🔁 3. Learned Triggers (Adaptive)

Alice learns patterns and proposes automation.

---

### Examples

- repeated manual tasks  
- consistent workflows  
- patterns across completed work  

---

### Behavior

- suggests turning patterns into recurring tasks  
- may pre-stage actions  
- never executes without user approval initially  

---

### Law

> Alice may suggest automation, but the user authorizes it.

---

## 🧠 Trigger Hierarchy

---

### Highest Control

User-defined triggers  

---

### Medium Control

User-approved learned triggers  

---

### Lowest Control

Suggestions only  

---

---

## ⚠️ Execution Rules

---

### Allowed

- executing scheduled tasks  
- suggesting actions  
- proposing automation  

---

### Not Allowed

- executing new behavior without approval  
- modifying existing triggers silently  
- escalating scope  

---

### Law

> No new automation without user consent.

---

## 🧠 Messaging Behavior

---

### Chat Suggestions

Alice may:

- recommend actions  
- suggest automation  
- offer improvements  

---

### Notifications

Alice may notify when:

- a scheduled task runs  
- a pattern is detected  
- a useful automation is available  

---

### Constraint

- must be relevant  
- must be minimal  
- must not create noise  

---

### Law

> Messages must provide value, not presence.

---

## 🔒 Relationship to Delegator

Delegator ensures:

- only allowed Talents run  
- only approved triggers execute  
- no deviation occurs
