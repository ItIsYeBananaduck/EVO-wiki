## Concept

Execution defines **how Alice carries out work**.

All execution is governed by:

- Methods (proposed plans)  
- Talents (strict execution lanes)  
- Delegator (enforcement)  

---

## Execution Types

### Method
- flexible plan  
- requires approval  
- must be followed exactly once approved  

---

### Talent
- predefined execution path  
- no deviation allowed  
- strict control over actions  

---

## Execution Rules

- no improvisation  
- no additional steps  
- no scope expansion  
- no external reinterpretation  

---

### 🔑 Law

> Follow the Method or stop.

---

## Deviation Handling

If execution deviates:

- execution is terminated  
- Delegator resets control  
- new Method is required  
- re-approval may be required  

---

## Execution Flow

1. user intent  
2. Method or Talent selected  
3. Delegator validates  
4. execution begins  
5. each step validated  
6. complete or terminate
