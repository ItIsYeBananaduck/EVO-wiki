# Authority vs Influence

## Concept
Authority defines who may decide. Influence defines who actually affects the outcome.

## Rule / Mechanism
A component may have high authority but zero influence if relevance is zero.

## Why It Exists
Prevents blind trust in components without context or experience.

## Implications
- User LoRA does not hallucinate control
- Safety dominates early phases
