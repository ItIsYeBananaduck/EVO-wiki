  

# Talent Promotion Consent

## Concept
Talent promotion is a separate user decision.

## Rule / Mechanism
When a method meets the promotion threshold:
- Alice proposes promotion
- the user must explicitly approve promotion
- only then is the method created as a Talent

## Why It Exists
Users must knowingly grant “no re-approval required” status.

## Implications
- Promotion is transparent
- Users retain control over autonomy
