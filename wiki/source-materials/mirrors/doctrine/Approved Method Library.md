# Approved Method Library

## Concept
Approved methods for non-Talent tasks are stored for reuse.

## Rule / Mechanism
When a user approves a method for a task (and the task executes successfully), the method is saved in an Approved Method Library.

Approved methods are reusable references that can be attached to future tasks.

## Why It Exists
Reduces repeated planning work while preserving explicit consent and safety.

## Implications
- Reuse is faster but not autonomous
- Delegator can point Alice to an existing approved method
- Approved methods are not Talents until promoted
