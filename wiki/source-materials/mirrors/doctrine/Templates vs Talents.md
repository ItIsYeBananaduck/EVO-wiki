# Learn – Templates vs Talents

## Templates

Templates are structured learning methods.

They define:
- How information is presented
- How it is practiced
- How it is reinforced
- How retention is tested

Templates are NOT workflows.
They are pedagogical approaches.

Examples:
- Memorization-based repetition
- Concept-first visualization
- Analogy-driven instruction
- Socratic questioning
- Step-by-step procedural reinforcement

---

## Talents (Learn Domain)

Talents orchestrate templates.

A Talent may:
- Apply a template to a specific lesson
- Run a micro-lesson
- Trigger reinforcement
- Conduct a retention probe

Templates = learning method  
Talents = execution logic using that method

---

Templates only exist in EVOlearn.
Talents exist across the ecosystem.
