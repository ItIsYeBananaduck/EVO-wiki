# Risk-Weighted Adaptation
[Risk-Weighted Adaptation](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad013819eabb4c59900b06708)
Concept
Adaptation decisions are weighted by injury and fatigue risk, not just performance.
Rule / Mechanism
Higher-risk variables require stronger confidence signals.
Low-risk variables may adapt with weaker signal.
Why It Exists
Not all progress signals are worth the same risk.
Implications
Conservative early behavior
Aggressive optimization only when earned
System remains user-aligned
