# MethodProfile Capture

## Inputs (fast)
Teacher answers:
- structure: I do / We do / You do OR other
- tone: calm/encouraging/energetic
- pacing: micro/standard/deep
- feedback style: gentle/direct/question-led
- common misconceptions (optional list)

## Inputs (rich, optional)
- upload 1-2 example lessons they like
- upload 1-2 example explanations they wrote
- transcribe one class session (optional)

## Output
MethodProfile used to wrap all LessonPacks for that teacher/class.
