# EVE – Procedure Governance

Procedures define:

- Ingestion scope
- KPI calculations
- Aggregation level
- Reporting format
- LoRA log channel

Only administrators may create or modify Procedures.

Department heads and managers may:
- View reports
- Request new procedures
- Propose changes

They cannot directly modify Procedure definitions.

---

# Rationale

Procedures shape organizational metrics.
Metric drift creates governance instability.

Administrative control ensures:
- Consistency
- Accountability
- Auditability
- Political neutrality
