# Communication Modes - Teacher Boundary Model
[Communication Modes – Teacher Boundary Model](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381c09aafd2bb5f346bfb)
EVOlearn supports multiple communication policies.

Mode A – Open Channel
Student may initiate teacher chat.
TA triages and screens.
Teacher manages quiet hours.
More flexible, higher load.

Mode B – AI-Gated Channel (Recommended Default)
Student cannot directly message teacher.
Student requests help from SA.
SA attempts resolution first.
SA escalates only when criteria met.
Escalations are structured tickets, not open chat.
Teacher receives: - concept tag - what was tried - structured summary - effort indicators
This mode: - protects teacher boundaries - prevents spam - maintains academic focus - scales better
