# Echo_4_UX_Hall
EVOmind — Echo: UX & Hall of Echoes
Hall of Echoes:
A peaceful environment where users reflect and access Echoes.

Experience Goals:
- Calm
- Grounding
- Intentional

Echo Library:
User may have multiple Echoes:
- Mom
- Dad
- Mentor

Each Echo is isolated.

Interaction Modes:

Voice:
- Avatar centered
- Presence-first

Text:
- Avatar shifts to side
- Chat bubbles primary

Transitions:
- Smooth movement between modes

Rules:
- No feed
- No gamification
- No rapid switching
- No blending Echoes

Core UX Principle:
Echo is a presence, not a tool.
