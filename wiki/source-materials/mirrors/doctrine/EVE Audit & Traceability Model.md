# EVE Audit & Traceability Model
Purpose:

Ensure all critical actions are traceable and reviewable.

Functions:

- log decisions and actions
- track overrides and exceptions
- enable audit trails across domains

Principle:

Every meaningful action should be explainable and reviewable.
