
## Core Principle

> Notes are not just connected — they are evaluated.

EVOconnect notes must express:

- relationships between concepts
- decision validity
- evolution of ideas over time

Alice uses this to:

- reason about architecture
- avoid outdated decisions
- prefer current sources of truth
- understand contradictions and tradeoffs

---

## 🔗 Relationship Types

All note-to-note links must express meaning, not just connection.

---

### Defines
This note establishes a concept.

Example:
- [[EVOconnect — Execution Model]]

---

### Uses
This note depends on another concept.

---

### Enforces
This note constrains another concept.

---

### Constrained By
This note is limited by another concept.

---

### Extends
This note builds on another concept.

---

### Implements
This note applies a concept in practice.

---

### Related
Loose relationship only.

---
