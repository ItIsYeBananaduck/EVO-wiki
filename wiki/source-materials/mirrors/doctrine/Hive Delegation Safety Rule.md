# Hive Delegation Safety Rule

## Concept
Delegation must not become an execution bypass.

## Rule / Mechanism
Delegated work is limited to:
- drafting
- summarization
- planning
- heavy inference that produces artifacts

Delegated work may not:
- execute tools with side effects
- resolve secrets
- approve methods
- promote Talents

All authorization decisions remain on the lease holder device.

## Why It Exists
Prevents the Hive from turning into distributed uncontrolled autonomy.

## Implications
- Delegation is compute-sharing, not authority-sharing
- Delegator remains the choke point
