# EVOlearn Difficulty Scaling Logic
Purpose:

Defines how challenge level is adjusted.

Signals:

- correctness
- confidence
- response speed
- repeated success or struggle

Behavior:

- raise difficulty gradually on mastery
- reduce complexity when struggle persists
- stabilize when challenge is appropriate

Principle:

Difficulty should stretch the learner without overwhelming them.
