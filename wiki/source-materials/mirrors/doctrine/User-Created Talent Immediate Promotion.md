# User-Created Talent Immediate Promotion
[User-Created Talent Immediate Promotion](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad01381c8830ec2bf39290879)
Concept
A Talent created through Talent Trainer is eligible for immediate promotion.
Rule / Mechanism
If a method is produced via Talent Trainer and passes the co-piloted verification run: - the user may approve immediate promotion to Talent - the 3-run threshold is not required
Why It Exists
The Talent Trainer workflow is already a safety demonstration and explicit consent mechanism.
Implications
Faster user-owned automation
No marketplace needed
Trust is still explicit and testable
