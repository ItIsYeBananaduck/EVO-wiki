## Core Principle

> A Talent is a Method that has been proven stable and can execute automatically without user approval.

---

## Promotion Rule

A Method becomes a Talent only when:

- Alice executes it  
- the user confirms success  
- this happens **3 times consecutively**  

---

## Definition

A Talent guarantees:

- repeatable execution  
- consistent outcomes  
- safe automation  

---

## Key Distinction

> Methods require approval  
> Talents do not  

---

## Automation Rule

Once promoted:

- Talents can run automatically  
- no step-by-step approval required  

---

## Drift Handling

If a Talent produces a different outcome:

- do NOT modify the Talent  
- create a new Method instead  

---

## Evolution Model

Old Talent remains intact  
New Method is proposed  
New Method can become a new Talent
