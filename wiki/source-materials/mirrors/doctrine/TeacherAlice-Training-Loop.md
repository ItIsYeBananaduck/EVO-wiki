# Teacher Alice Training Loop

Inputs:
- lesson plans
- lecture transcripts (optional)
- teacher annotations ("my goal here", "common misconceptions")
- preferred teaching structure (I do → We do → You do, etc.)

Outputs:
- packaged lesson units
- multi-level explanations
- practice banks
- safe game-template configurations

Teacher approval:
- teacher can lock outputs
- teacher can approve variants
- teacher can disable open-ended generation
