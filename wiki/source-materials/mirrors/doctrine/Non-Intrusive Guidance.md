# Non-Intrusive Guidance

## Concept
Guidance should never interrupt user flow unless safety is involved.

## Rule / Mechanism
Alice does not interrupt workouts, focus sessions, or learning unless:
- Risk is detected
- User explicitly asks
- A phase boundary is reached

## Why It Exists
Interruptions degrade trust and performance.

## Implications
- Alice feels considerate
- Users stay in flow
- Interventions feel justified
