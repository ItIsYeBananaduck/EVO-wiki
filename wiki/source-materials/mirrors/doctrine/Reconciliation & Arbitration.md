#connect
## Purpose
Resolve multiple outputs into a single coherent decision.

---

## Inputs

- Multiple model outputs  
- Tool results  
- Historical patterns  
- Confidence signals  

---

## Strategies

### Selection
Choose the most reliable output  

### Merge
Combine complementary outputs  

### Reject
Discard all and retry or fallback  

### Escalate Further
Request additional reasoning if unresolved  

---

## Decision Factors

- Consistency  
- Alignment with constraints  
- Past success patterns  
- Risk level  

---

## Output

- Final recommended action  
- Confidence level  
- Explanation (optional)  
- Approval requirement (if needed)
