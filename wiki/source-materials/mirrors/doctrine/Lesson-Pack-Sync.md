# Lesson-Pack-Sync
Lesson Pack Sync (Teacher Alice → Student Alice)
Overview
Teacher Alice (TA) distributes lesson plans as structured Lesson Packs to all Student Alices (SA). Student Alices cache packs locally for offline use.
Delivery
TA publishes Lesson Pack(s) to the class.
Each SA receives the full pack set for the active unit.
Packs include all lesson content, but access is gated by unlock rules.
Offline
Packs are cached on student devices.
Student can work without internet at home.
Progress sync occurs when connectivity resumes.
Unlock Gating
Lessons are time/progress bounded.
Student cannot unlock next lesson until current lesson completion criteria are met.
Teacher can override gates (manual unlock).
SA Personalization Allowed
Within the currently unlocked lesson only, SA may: - adjust explanation style (visual/step-by-step/story) - adjust pacing (micro-chunks, more practice) - generate extra practice variants from teacher-approved templates - provide hints and retries
SA Personalization Not Allowed
SA may NOT: - skip gated lesson sequencing by default - introduce off-curriculum topics in Kids mode - rewrite teacher objectives - unlock future lessons without meeting criteria or teacher override
