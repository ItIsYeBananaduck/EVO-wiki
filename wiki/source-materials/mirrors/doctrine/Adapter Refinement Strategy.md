## Purpose
Improve Alice’s performance without copying external models.

---

## What Adapters Should Improve

- Escalation detection  
- Context packaging quality  
- Task decomposition  
- Conflict recognition  
- Output structuring  
- Workflow efficiency  

---

## What Adapters Should NOT Do

- Copy external model reasoning wholesale  
- Override governance rules  
- Introduce unverified behavior  
- Replace core system logic  

---

## Philosophy

Adapters enhance how Alice works, not what she is.
