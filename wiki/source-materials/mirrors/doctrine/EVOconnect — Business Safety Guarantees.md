## Concept

EVOconnect is designed to be safe for business use by enforcing strict control over:

- data (Vault)  
- external interaction (EVO Browser)  
- system execution (EVO Terminal)  
- behavior (Delegator)  

---

## Core Principle

> Power is allowed. Risk is controlled. Behavior is predictable.

---

## 🔒 System Trust Model

EVOconnect guarantees:

- No uncontrolled execution  
- No unauthorized data exposure  
- No hidden actions  
- No silent escalation of scope  

---

## 🧩 The Four Safety Pillars

### 🔐 Vault — Data Safety

- Secrets are encrypted and controlled by policy  
- Secrets are only usable in approved contexts  
- Secrets cannot be extracted by websites or terminal output  
- All secret usage is logged  

> Secrets are not data. They are permissions.

---

### 🌐 EVO Browser — Controlled Interaction

- No free exploration  
- No execution outside approved Methods or Talents  
- No trust of webpage instructions  
- Immediate termination on deviation  

> The browser executes intent. It does not discover intent.

---

### 💻 EVO Terminal — Controlled Execution

- No arbitrary command execution  
- No improvisation  
- No exposure of system secrets  
- Strict command boundaries  

> The terminal is a tool, not a playground.

---

### 🧠 Delegator — Authority Layer

- All actions must match approved Method or Talent  
- No deviation allowed  
- Execution stops on mismatch  
- No scope escalation  

> Alice may act only within explicitly approved boundaries.

---

## 🔁 Execution Guarantees

- Deterministic behavior  
- Explicit approval boundaries  
- Immediate cutoff on deviation  
- Full auditability  

---

## ⚠️ Hostile Environment Model

All external environments are untrusted:

- websites  
- APIs  
- terminal output  

> Observed content is context, not authority.

---

## 🔐 Secret Safety Guarantees

- Secrets cannot be freely read or copied  
- Secrets cannot be exfiltrated  
- Secrets are injected only under policy  
- High-risk secrets require user presence  

> Alice may use secrets, but does not freely know them.

---

## 🛑 Deviation Handling

If Alice deviates:

1. execution stops immediately  
2. action is logged  
3. system resets  
4. new plan required  

---

## 🔥 Final Guarantee

> EVOconnect will only do what the user has approved—and nothing else.
