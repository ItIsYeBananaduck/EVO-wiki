# Deferred Response Strategy

## Concept
Not all insights need to be delivered immediately.

## Rule / Mechanism
Insights generated during background or live activity contexts are queued.

They are surfaced only when the user is receptive.

## Why It Exists
Timing matters as much as accuracy.

## Implications
- Better comprehension
- Less cognitive overload
- Higher trust in recommendations
