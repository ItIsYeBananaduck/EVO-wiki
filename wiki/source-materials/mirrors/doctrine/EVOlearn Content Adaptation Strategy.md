# EVOlearn Content Adaptation Strategy
Purpose:

Defines how learning content is adapted over time.

Inputs:

- performance trends
- retention signals
- preferred content type

Behavior:

- surface more support where needed
- vary explanation depth
- adjust presentation style

Principle:

Content should adapt to the learner, not force the learner to adapt to the content.
