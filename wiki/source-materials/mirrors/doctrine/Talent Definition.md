# Talent Definition

## Concept
A Talent is a user-defined, repeatable action that has been proven safe through prior execution.

## Rule / Mechanism
A Talent is created by promoting a previously approved method after successful use.

Talents represent earned trust, not assumed capability.

## Why It Exists
Talents allow safe automation without repeated approvals.

## Implications
- Talents are explicit artifacts
- Talents are bounded by their original method definition
- Talent behavior is predictable and auditable
