# EVOlearn MOC

## Purpose
EVOlearn is an adaptive education system designed to scale with the user over time.

It emphasizes understanding, engagement, and long-term growth.

---

## Core Principles
Constraints that will govern all learning behavior.

- [[On-Device First Principle]]
- [[Presence by Value]]
- [[Silence as a Feature]]
- [[Authority vs Influence]]

---

## Intended Direction
EVOlearn is expected to support:

- Game-based learning (early stages)
- Guided exploration
- Skill reinforcement
- Adaptive pacing

Exact implementations are undecided.

---

## Age & Maturity Scaling
Learning experience should evolve with the user.

- Younger users: interaction-first, playful
- Older users: concept-first, reflective
- Adults: efficiency and clarity

---

## Alice’s Role in Learning
Alice acts as:

- Teacher
- Guide
- Feedback partner

Not as:
- Examiner
- Authority figure
- Enforcer

## Architectural Rule
Learning should adapt to the user.
The user should not adapt to the system.
