# Live Activity Inference

## Concept
Live activities allow ongoing intelligence without foreground UI.

## Rule / Mechanism
During live activities:
- Inference is allowed
- UI updates are minimal
- Interruptions are suppressed unless safety-critical

## Why It Exists
Users may be active without actively looking at the app.

## Implications
- Pocket-safe operation
- Workout-aware intelligence
- Battery-conscious behavior
