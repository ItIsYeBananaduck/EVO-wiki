# Rest Recommendation Policy
[Rest Recommendation Policy](https://www.notion.so/33ec72bad013814e822fec0eb7b83b17)
Concept
Rest recommendations must be derived from strain and goal context while remaining conservative.
Rule / Mechanism
Rest recommendations prioritize safety and performance consistency over aggression.
Rest recommendations do not modify the training plan.
Why It Exists
Rest is a tactical lever during the session; it should not cause strategic drift.
Implications
Rest can adapt without changing sets/reps/load
Explaining rest is optional unless asked
