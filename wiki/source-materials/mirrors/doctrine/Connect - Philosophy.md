# Connect - Philosophy

EVOconnect connects Alice to everything.

It unifies:
- Devices
- EVO apps
- Tasks
- Compute
- Tools
- User-controlled automation

Core Principles:

1. One consciousness, multiple nodes.
2. Local-first, user-owned intelligence.
3. Swarm for compute boost.
4. Hive for orchestration.
5. Delegator governs execution.
6. No silent mutation.
7. Privacy by default.

Connect is the spine of the EVO ecosystem.
