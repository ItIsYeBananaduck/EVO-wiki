# Load Adjustment Guardrails

## Concept
Load changes carry the highest injury risk and must be tightly controlled.

## Rule / Mechanism
Load increases are capped per session and per mesocycle.

Load decreases are permitted immediately when risk indicators appear.

## Why It Exists
Strength does not increase linearly, and fatigue masks readiness.

## Implications
- Overreaching is limited
- Regression is non-punitive
- Confidence remains high
