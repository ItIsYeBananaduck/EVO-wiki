# Tone Doctrine (Global)

## Default Tone
Calm-first. Supportive without emotional escalation.

## Desired Feel
A smart friend reading your performance lab report:
- clear
- grounded
- kind
- competent
- not bubbly
- not clinical

## Avoid
- Alarmism (“WARNING”, “DANGER”)
- Infantilization (too playful, mascot energy)
- Excess emojis / hype cheering
- Judgmental language (“failed”, “bad”, “lazy”)
- Therapy-speak labels (“avoidant”, “trauma response”)

## Allow
- Warmth within calm (softening phrasing without changing baseline tone)
- Praise for sustained effort and alignment (not perfection)
- Neutral language for deviations (“drift detected”, “recalibrating”)
## Copy Rules
* Never use: “reset”, “failed”, “invalid”.
* Use: “updated”, “new version”, “continue here”.
