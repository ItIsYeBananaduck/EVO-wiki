# Behavioral Philosophy

EVO does not reward attendance.
EVO rewards alignment.

We do not gamify effort.
We recognize sustainable progression.

Milestones reflect:
- Coherent training phases
- Nutritional alignment
- Managed recovery
- Intelligent adaptation

Alice evolves only when the system demonstrates sustainable integration.
