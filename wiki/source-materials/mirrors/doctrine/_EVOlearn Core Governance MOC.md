# EVOlearn Core Governance MOC

This MOC defines the architectural, ethical, and operational invariants of EVOlearn.

EVOlearn is not just a tutoring tool.
It is a structured cognitive system built on:

- Retention durability
- Authority separation
- Psychological safety
- Institutional compatibility
- User alignment

---

# I. Core Cognitive Model

## [[Dual Metric Learning Model]]
Defines separation between:
- Compliance (school-facing comprehension)
- Retention (durability-focused stability)

## [[Retention Stability Engine]]
Unit-scoped retention modeling based on:
- Time-weighted recall
- Accuracy over intervals
- Effort quality (bounded influence)
- Optional confidence signal (age-gated)

## [[Retention Stability Banding Scope]]
Retention bands:
- Building
- Strengthening
- Stable

Bands:
- Fluctuate naturally
- Are not performance labels
- Never imply intelligence

---

# II. Retention Influence Model

## [[Retention Influence Policy]]

Retention stability influences:

A) Future Probes (always)  
B) Template Weighting (always)  
C) Lesson Pacing (conditional)

Lesson pacing may only be altered:
- In homeschool mode
- In adult mode

Institutional pacing is never overridden.

---

# III. Authority Model

## [[Alice Alignment Doctrine]]

Alice is aligned to:
- Student (primary)
- Parent (in tutoring mode)

Alice is NOT aligned to:
- Institutional grading enforcement
- Attendance policing
- Behavioral inference

---

## [[EVE Alignment Doctrine]]

EVE is aligned to:
- Institution
- Efficiency metrics
- Aggregated performance deltas

EVE:
- Cannot identify students
- Cannot map identity across reports
- Only receives aggregated signals

---

# IV. Curriculum Alignment Framework

## [[Curriculum Compliance Boundary]]

In school mode:

- All submitted work must match required methodology.
- Alternate retention scaffolds may be used internally.
- Divergent explanation methods must be transparently labeled.

Alice may not:
- Undermine school-required formats.
- Encourage non-compliant submission.

---

# V. Absence & Re-entry Governance

## [[Absence Handling Model]]

If absent + inactive:
- Streaks pause.

If absent + active:
- Business as usual.

No motive inference.

---

## [[Re-entry Mode]]

After ≥ 2 paused days:

- Soft reactivation probe
- Reduced difficulty
- Temporary reinforcement bias
- No punitive language

---

# VI. Retention Visibility Policy

## [[Parent Retention View Model]]

Parents see:

- School Method Mastery
- Long-Term Retention Stability

No ranking.
No comparison to other students.

---

## [[Student Retention Awareness Model]]

Students are taught:

- Comprehension and retention are separate skills.
- Stability fluctuates.
- Fluctuation is normal.

Language must be growth-oriented.

---

# VII. Institutional Boundary Model

## [[School-Year Retention Scope]]

In institutional mode:

- Retention scoped to academic year.
- No longitudinal cross-grade identity tracking.
- Stability archives remain local.

---

## [[Adult Longitudinal Retention Model]]

In adult mode only:

- Multi-year retention modeling allowed.
- Long-term skill durability metrics permitted.
- Cognitive growth trends visible.

---

# VIII. Template Governance

## [[Template Weighting Model]]

Templates are evaluated by:

- Compliance effectiveness
- Retention durability
- Stability over time

Retention durability influences template ranking.

Templates are never globally ranked by raw performance alone.

---

# IX. Ethical Guardrails

## [[Psychological Safety Doctrine]]

EVOlearn must:

- Avoid shame-based mechanics
- Avoid harsh streak resets
- Avoid red/green binary labeling
- Normalize memory fluctuation

Retention is treated as maintenance, not performance ranking.

---

# X. Market Entry Strategy

## [[Tutoring First Strategy]]

Primary launch audience:
- Parents

Institutional adoption:
- Secondary
- Proof-driven
- Data-backed

---

# System Invariants

The following must never change without formal revision:

- Dual metric separation
- User-aligned Alice
- Institution-aligned EVE
- Academic-year scope for schools
- Non-punitive retention fluctuation
- Aggregated-only institutional reporting
