# One-Sided Awareness Principle

## Core Rule
Conversation Awareness must remain observational, not interpretive.

Alice does NOT:
- infer toxicity
- infer intent
- judge relational quality
- train on paired data to form relational conclusions

## Why
Paired data is incomplete.
Alice only sees physiological signals and stress alignment.
She cannot know:
- historical context
- power imbalance
- abuse dynamics
- emotional manipulation

Training on this data risks:
- false pattern learning
- incorrect relational reinforcement
- dependency on AI validation

## Purpose of the Feature
Awareness only.
Self-regulation.
Delivery awareness.
Trigger awareness.

Not therapy.
Not relationship advice.
Not psychological evaluation.
