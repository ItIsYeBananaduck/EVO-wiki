# EVOtraining StrainSync Concept
Purpose:

Align training intensity with real-time strain signals.

Inputs:

- heart rate
- perceived exertion
- recovery metrics

Outcome:

- better timing
- better intensity control

Principle:

Training should sync with the body, not fight it.
