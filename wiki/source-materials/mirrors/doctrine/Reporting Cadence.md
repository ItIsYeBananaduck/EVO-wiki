# Reporting Cadence
EVOlearn Reporting Cadence
Official Reporting Cycle
EVOlearn aligns with school grading periods (~4–5 weeks).

Continuous (Internal Only)
Student App: - emits micro telemetry continuously - stored locally
TA: - aggregates in real time - provides class-level insight - does not escalate upward during grading period

End-of-Cycle Escalation
At grading period end:
TA → Cohort Summary SE → School Summary DE → District Summary
EVE runs Procedures only on aggregated cycle data.

Rationale
Aligns with school systems
Prevents overreaction to weekly variance
Reduces compute load
Maintains metric stability
Builds institutional trust
