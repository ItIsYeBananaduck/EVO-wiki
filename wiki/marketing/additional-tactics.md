## Tactics We Haven't Covered Yet

### 1. App Store Optimization (ASO) — CRITICAL

This is the most important one we haven't discussed. When someone searches "workout app" or "AI trainer" in the App Store or Google Play, you need to show up.

| Element | What to optimize |
|---|---|
| **App name** | "EVOtraining: AI Trainer" (include keyword) |
| **Subtitle** | "Adaptive workouts that learn from real trainers" |
| **Keywords** | workout, fitness, AI trainer, personal trainer, gym, exercise, adaptation |
| **Screenshots** | Show Alice adapting, before/after, marketplace |
| **Description** | Lead with value prop, include keywords naturally |
| **Ratings** | Ask beta users to rate early (social proof) |
| **Icon** | Distinctive, recognizable at small sizes |

**Why this matters:** ASO is free, passive, and compounds. A top-10 ranking for "AI trainer" could drive 100+ downloads/day without any ongoing effort.

**Action:** Research competitors' keywords. Use App Store Connect's keyword field wisely (100 characters).

---

### 2. Product Hunt Launch

**What:** Launch EVOtraining on Product Hunt (producthunt.com)
**When:** Launch day (Jan 1) or shortly after
**Why:** Tech audience, early adopters, press attention

**How to prepare:**
- Create a Product Hunt profile
- Write a compelling tagline
- Prepare screenshots + demo video
- Line up supporters (friends, beta users, Kevin Miller)
- Engage in comments on launch day

**Potential impact:** 500–2,000 visits on launch day, press pickup, backlinks.

---

### 3. Podcast Appearances

**Target podcasts:**
- Fitness: "The Model Health Show", "Mind Pump", "Fitness Disrupted"
- Tech/AI: "Lex Fridman", "All-In", "Acquired" (long shot)
- Indie hacker: "Indie Hackers", "Startups for the Rest of Us"
- Local podcasts (easier to get on)

**Pitch:**
> "I'm a solo dev who built an AI trainer that learns from real trainers. I was embarrassed to hire a trainer, so I built one. Happy to share the journey."

**Why:** Free credibility, backlinks, audience trust. One podcast = weeks of content repurposing.

---

### 4. Guest Posting

**Target blogs:**
- Fitness: Bodybuilding.com, Men's Health, Shape, ACE Fitness
- Tech: Hacker Noon, freeCodeCamp, Medium publications
- Indie: Indie Hackers, Dev.to

**Topics:**
- "I Built an AI Trainer Because I Was Embarrassed to Hire One"
- "Why 91% of Workout Plans Fail (And How AI Fixes This)"
- "How Federated Learning Makes AI Fitness Apps Private"

**Why:** Free backlinks (SEO), authority building, referral traffic.

---

### 5. Referral Program

**How it works:**
- Existing users get a unique referral link
- When someone signs up via their link, both get 1 month free
- Built into the app (or manual at first)

**Why:** Free acquisition channel. If 100 users refer 2 friends each = 200 new users at $0 cost.

**Implementation:**
- Simple landing page: `evotraining.app/ref/USERNAME`
- Track signups manually at first
- Automate later

---

### 6. Email Signature Marketing

**What:** Add a line to Phil's email signature:
> "I built EVOtraining — an AI trainer that learns from real trainers. Join the waitlist: evotraining.app"

**Why:** Every email becomes marketing. Free, passive, reaches people who already know Phil.

---

### 7. Startup Directories

**Where to list (all free):**
- BetaList.com
- AlternativeTo.net
- Product Hunt (pre-launch page)
- AngelList/Wellfound
- Crunchbase
- Startup Stash

**Why:** Backlinks, discovery, credibility. One-time setup, long-term benefit.

---

### 8. Quora

**How it works:**
- Answer questions about gym anxiety, workout plans, AI fitness
- Provide genuine value
- Mention EVOtraining naturally when relevant

**Example questions:**
- "How do I start working out if I'm embarrassed?"
- "What's the best AI fitness app?"
- "Why do most workout plans fail?"

**Why:** High-intent traffic, long-term SEO value, establishes authority.

---

### 9. YouTube Long-Form

**Content ideas:**
- "I Built an AI Trainer — Here's How It Works" (dev log)
- "Why 91% of Resolutions Fail" (education)
- "EVOtraining vs. Trainerize vs. MyFitnessPal" (comparison)

**Why:** YouTube is the second-largest search engine. Long-form content ranks for years. Repurpose into TikToks/Reels.

---

### 10. Cross-Promotion with Other Indie Devs

**How it works:**
- Find other indie devs with complementary products
- Swap newsletter mentions, social shoutouts, or bundle deals
- Example: Productivity app + fitness app = "Body + Mind" bundle

**Why:** Free access to aligned audiences. No money changes hands.

---

### 11. Strategic Giveaways

**How it works:**
- "Share this post + follow us = win 1 year free"
- "Tag 3 friends who want to get in shape = win early access"

**Why:** Viral loop. Low cost (free subscriptions), high reach.

**Caution:** Don't overdo it. Giveaways attract freebies-seekers, not necessarily long-term users.

---

### 12. HARO (Help a Reporter Out)

**What:** Journalists query HARO for sources. You respond with expertise.
**Why:** Free press. One mention in a major publication = thousands of visits.

**How:**
- Sign up for HARO (free)
- Monitor queries for "fitness," "AI," "New Year's resolutions"
- Respond with Phil's story: "Solo dev builds AI trainer after experiencing gym anxiety"
