## Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors

| App | Price | What They Do | What They Lack |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Runna** | ~$14.99/mo | AI running plans | No trainers, no strength, no marketplace |
| **PUSH** | ~$14.99/mo | Strength tracking | No trainers, no collective intelligence |
| **Fitbudd** | ~$15/mo | AI workout generation | No trainers, no marketplace |
| **Trainerize** | $0–$275/mo | B2B trainer software | No AI for end-users |
| **Nike Training Club** | Free | Workout library | No personalization, no adaptation |

## The White Space

> **"An AI trainer that learns from every trainer, adapts to every user, and never judges."**

No one owns this space. The AI fitness apps are trainer-less. The trainer platforms (Trainerize) are AI-less. EVO is both.
