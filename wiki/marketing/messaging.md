# Messaging

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| **Authentic** | We're a solo dev building after our 9-5. No corporate polish. |
| **Empathetic** | We understand gym anxiety because we've felt it. |
| **Intelligent** | We explain complex things simply. We respect our users. |
| **Direct** | No fluff. No jargon. Just the truth. |
| **Hopeful** | We believe in the 9% who don't give up. |

### What We Sound Like

**Do:**
- "Alice learns from every trainer and adapts to you."
- "No judgment. Just results."
- "We're building this after our 9-5. We get it."

**Don't:**
- "Revolutionize your fitness journey."
- "Leverage cutting-edge AI technology."
- "Transform your body in 30 days."

## Key Messages

### The Problem
> *"50% of Americans experience gym anxiety. 70% of beginners feel intimidated. You're not alone. You're the majority."*

### The Solution
> *"Alice is the AI trainer that learns from real trainers and adapts to you. She doesn't judge. She adapts."*

### The Product
> *"EVOtraining is a living training system. Not a plan generator. Alice adjusts your sets, reps, and load based on your feedback."*

### The Ecosystem
> *"EVOtraining is chapter one. EVO is the ecosystem — training, mind, learning, connect. One subscription. One AI."*

### The Differentiator
> *"Trainerize charges trainers up to $275/mo. EVOtraining pays trainers to grow. We make money when you succeed."*

### The Privacy
> *"All personal data stays on your device. No centralized store. No silent telemetry. Your data is yours."*

### The Future
> *"Alice grows with you from childhood to lifelong partner. One day, she'll represent you. One day, she'll be your legacy."*

## Platform-Specific Messaging

### TikTok / Reels
- Raw, authentic, quick
- "POV: You finally stopped caring what people think at the gym"
- "I built an AI trainer because I was embarrassed to hire one"
- "She adapts when I'm tired. She pushes when I'm ready."

### Instagram
- Visual, aspirational, educational
- Before/after Alice adaptations
- Quick fitness tips
- BTS of building EVO

### Facebook
- Community-focused, longer-form
- Dev logs
- Trainer spotlights
- Marketplace previews

### Reddit
- Genuine, helpful, non-promotional
- "I built an AI trainer that adapts to you — here's what happened"
- "Why most workout plans fail (and how to fix them)"
