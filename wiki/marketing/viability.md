# Viability Assessment

## Revenue Targets

| Milestone | Users | MRR | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Break-even infra** | ~70 users | ~$1,000 | Month 1–3 |
| **Sustainable side project** | 200 users | $3,000 | Month 6 |
| **Phil goes full-time** | 700+ users | $10,000+ | Month 12–18 |

## Success Probabilities

| Outcome | Probability | What It Looks Like |
|---|---|---|
| **Failure (shut down < 12 months)** | 40–50% | < 100 users, Phil burns out |
| **Modest success (side project)** | 30–35% | 100–300 users, $1.5k–$4.5k MRR |
| **Real business (Phil goes full-time)** | 15–20% | 700+ users, $10k+ MRR |
| **Breakout success** | 5–10% | Viral moment, 5,000+ users |

## Critical Path to Survival

### Month 1–2: Foundation
- [ ] Deploy landing page to evotraining.app
- [ ] Create social accounts (@evotraining)
- [ ] Get Kevin Miller into beta + testimonial
- [ ] Shoot 5–10 pieces of content

### Month 3–4: Traction
- [ ] 500 waitlist emails
- [ ] 3–5 trainers in marketplace
- [ ] 50–100 beta users
- [ ] $500–$1,000 MRR

### Month 5–6: Validation
- [ ] 200+ paying users
- [ ] 3,000+ social followers
- [ ] $3,000+ MRR
- [ ] Kevin + 2 trainers bringing clients

## Kill Criteria

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| < 50 waitlist signups after 30 days of content | Message isn't resonating. Pivot positioning. |
| Kevin Miller says no or ghosts | Find another wedge. Cold outreach only. |
| Zero trainers join after 60 days | Marketplace concept is broken. Focus on AI-only. |
| < 5% engagement rate on social content | Content quality or audience mismatch. Adjust. |
| Phil burns out from 9-5 + EVO grind | Pause marketing, maintain existing users only. |

## What "Success" Realistically Looks Like

### Best Case (12 months)
- 1,000 paying users
- $15,000 MRR
- Phil goes full-time on EVO
- 50+ trainers in marketplace

### Realistic Case (12 months)
- 200–400 paying users
- $3,000–$6,000 MRR
- Phil stays at 9-5, EVO is meaningful side income
- 10–15 trainers in marketplace

### Worst Case (12 months)
- < 50 paying users
- < $1,000 MRR
- Phil burns out, shelves EVO

## The Verdict

**Is EVOtraining viable? Yes, but with caveats.**

The product is differentiated. The market is real. The audience is validated. The pricing works.

But the go-to-market is the bottleneck. With $0 budget, solo founder, and time constraints, growth will be linear and slow unless a content piece goes viral or a trainer brings a large client base.

**Recommendation:** Treat this as a side project for 12 months. Set clear milestones. If you hit $3k MRR by Month 6–9, consider going full-time. If not, keep the 9-5 and maintain EVO as a passion project.
