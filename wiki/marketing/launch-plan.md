# Launch Plan

## Timeline

| Phase | Timeline | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** | Now–Sept 2026 | Brand building, landing page, social accounts |
| **Alpha** | Sept 2026 | Internal testing, bug fixes |
| **Beta** | Oct–Dec 2026 | Trainer recruitment, small user cohort |
| **Marketing Ramp** | Nov–Dec 2026 | Content blitz, waitlist push, early-bird offer |
| **Launch** | Jan 1, 2027 | New Year's Day resolution window |

## Content Sequencing Decision

**Video content does not start until Beta.** Phil dogfoods the app first — the adaptation loop must have demonstrably worked on a real person over real weeks before any demo footage is produced. Marketing a claim instead of a product reads as inauthentic to fitness audiences, and adaptation footage cannot be faked.

What this splits into:

| Can run before Beta | Requires Beta first |
|---|---|
| Landing page live (no dated promises) | Video demos of Alice adapting |
| Build-in-public text posts — documents the journey, not results | Before/after content |
| Kevin Miller outreach — he is a tester, not a customer | Testimonials |
| ASO keyword research, startup directory listings | Any "it works" claim |

**Accepted risk** `inferred`: video starting in October leaves roughly ten weeks before the resolution window. Short-form platforms tend to reward accounts with established posting history, so a cold account is unlikely to have algorithmic momentum by January 1. Magnitude of that ramp is unverified. Consequence: trainer recruitment becomes the load-bearing acquisition channel rather than a supporting one.

## Phase 1: Foundation (Now–September)

### Goals
- Deploy landing page to evotraining.app — no dated launch promise on it
- Reserve social handles (@evotraining on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook)
- Build-in-public text posts only — no product demo content
- Kevin Miller outreach
- ASO keyword research, startup directory listings

### Milestones
| Week | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 1 | Landing page live, handles reserved |
| 2 | Kevin Miller contacted |
| 3 | Listed on BetaList + AlternativeTo |
| 4 | ASO keywords researched |

### Why the page goes up now
The page is the destination for anything that earns attention — build-in-public posts, word of mouth, directory listings. Without it live, that attention evaporates. It carries no date, so it cannot break a promise.

**List decay** `inferred`: an email list left cold for months loses open rate and conversion. Mitigation is cadence, not delay — one email a month (dev log, screenshot, trainer story) keeps it warm and doubles as build-in-public material. Decay magnitude is unverified.

## Phase 2: Alpha (September)

### Goals
- Internal testing with Phil + small circle
- Phil dogfoods the adaptation loop — this is what unlocks video content later
- Bug fixes and polish
- No product-demo marketing activity

## Phase 3: Beta (October–December)

### Goals
- Recruit 5–10 trainers (starting with Kevin Miller)
- Onboard 50–100 beta users
- Validate marketplace concept
- Test payment flow
- **Video content begins** — the app has now proven itself on Phil, so demo footage is honest

### Trainer Strategy
| Trainer | Role |
|---|---|
| **Kevin Miller** (Iron Man Miller Fitness) | First beta trainer, testimonial, referrals |
| **5–10 others** | Marketplace supply, client acquisition |

### Beta Recruitment
- **No mass beta** — infra costs without revenue is unsustainable
- Focus on trainers who bring clients
- Each trainer = 5–10 users
- Target: 50–100 beta users by December

### Milestones
| Month | Milestone |
|---|---|
| October | Kevin Miller onboarded, first testimonial |
| November | 5 trainers in marketplace |
| December | 10 trainers, 100+ beta users |

## Phase 4: Marketing Ramp (November–December)

### Goals
- Content production at full speed
- Waitlist growth (1,000+ emails)
- Early-bird offer for launch

### Content Strategy
- 1–2 TikToks/day
- 1 Reel/day
- 3–5 Instagram Stories/day
- 3–5 Facebook posts/week
- 2–3 genuine Reddit comments/day

### Early-Bird Offer
- First 100 users get 50% off ($7.50/mo)
- Scarcity + urgency drives conversion

## Phase 5: Launch (January 1, 2027)

### Goals
- Waitlist gets early access
- Trainers promote to their client base
- Resolution-season press

### Launch Day Content
- Launch announcement across all platforms
- Trainer testimonials
- "New Year, New Trainer" campaign
- Email sequence to waitlist

## Trainer Outreach Template

### Text/DM
> "Hey [name] — I've been building something I think you'd appreciate. It's called EVOtraining — an AI trainer that learns from real trainers like you. Users find you through the marketplace, we handle payments, and you keep what you charge. Looking for founding trainers to beta test with in October. No cost, no commitment. Interested?"

### Key Points
- "You'd be in the marketplace" — visibility, not just software
- "We handle payments" — removes friction
- "You keep what you charge" — no cost to participate
- "Your intelligence feeds the AI" — expertise makes the product better
- "No cost, no commitment" — low barrier

### What to Avoid
- "It's better than Trainerize" — sounds competitive
- "You'll make money" — overpromises
- "I need you" — desperation
