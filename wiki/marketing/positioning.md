# Positioning

## The Problem

- **50% of Americans** experience some degree of gym anxiety
- **70% of beginners** report feeling intimidated at their first gym visit
- **60% cite embarrassment** as a barrier to going to the gym
- **91% of New Year's resolutions fail** within the first year
- **Average resolution lasts 3.74 months**

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Beginners too embarrassed to hire a trainer.

**Demographics:**
- Adults 18–45
- All fitness levels (beginner-focused)
- iOS and Android users
- North American market initially

**Psychographics:**
- Feel intimidated by gyms
- Self-conscious about being watched
- Want trainer-quality guidance without the judgment
- Value privacy and data ownership
- Open to AI-powered solutions

## Value Proposition

> *"Alice is the AI trainer that learns from every trainer. She adapts to you. She doesn't judge. She grows with you."*

### The Ecosystem Pitch

EVOtraining is the entry point. The real product is EVO — an AI ecosystem that grows with you from childhood to lifelong partnership.

| App | What Alice Establishes |
|---|---|
| **EVOtraining** | *Capability* — she can do useful things for you |
| **EVOconnect** | *Safety* — she can be trusted with governance and control |
| **EVOmind** | *Trust* — she knows you deeply, your patterns, your inner life |
| **EVOlearn** | *Growth* — she brings it all together, helps you become more |
| **EVOconnect Enterprise** | *Advocacy* — she represents you, defends you, works alongside you |

## The White Space

> **"An AI trainer that learns from every trainer, adapts to every user, and never judges."**

No one owns this space. The AI fitness apps are trainer-less. The trainer platforms (Trainerize) are AI-less. EVO is both.
